Two subsequent targeted editorial checks are included in those 8. Full suite was not repeated after the final transport-only changes. Real providers, desktop packages, multi-platform publishing and real-video visual quality were not validated.

Remaining blueprint work (not represented as implemented): retention scene mapping; true text-geometry/platform safe-area validation; platform export variants and export gating; measured silence snapping; within-shot speaker switching/audio correlation; claim-to-evidence/freshness validation; article section rewriting/diagrams; Lottie preview/export parity; multi-aspect rendering; loudness/peak and browser visual review; cursor/take tracking; Reels/TikTok CSV adapters and learning loop. Data/provider-dependent acceptance requires actual inputs and credentials supplied securely in environment settings.

Security controls reduce exposure; they are not a completed penetration test or a guarantee that credentials can never leak. Raw process memory, user-authored content and third-party applications need separate threat modeling. Existing local provider settings still contain secrets by design and must remain outside version control.
